My Music Playlist

Listening to music is one of my favorite hobbies because it has the incredible ability to connect with my emotions in ways words can't. No matter what I'm feeling, whether it's joy, sadness, or anything in between, there's always a perfect song that speaks to me.

This is a new song by Giveon called Twenties. I've had this on repeat ever since it was released.

This is Always, a song by Daniel Caesar. I like to listen to this at night when I get in my feels.

Frank Ocean's White Ferrari is a poignant song about love and loss. I listen to this when I'm going through tough emotions.
